✦The core idea

Hidento is an integrated software ecosystem for dental labs. It connects the lab with its doctors and with the technician and freelancer community on one platform and one database, instead of separate systems that don't talk to each other.

★What sets us apart
  • Three connected products on one platform
  • A smile designer for doctors (rare among competitors)
  • Multi-country invoicing and tax, including the Gulf
  • Full Arabic and English interface
  • Live demo, Excel import, weekly and monthly automations
