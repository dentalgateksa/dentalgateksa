Every feature of the platform on one page

A quick guide for anyone who will work on or take over the platform: what exists and what it does

64features

🦷Lab management (ERP)8

  1. 1Case intake and tracking: received → in progress → ready → delivered, with a full log of who changed what and when
  2. 2Live dashboard with the lab's key numbers
  3. 3A due date for every case and a daily alert for overdue cases
  4. 4Assign a case to a technician; technicians see only their own cases
  5. 5A chat on every case between the lab and the doctor
  6. 6Multi-branch: the admin sees all branches, branch staff see only theirs
  7. 7Role-based permissions: admin, branch manager, employee, HR, accountant, technician
  8. 8AI assistant that answers the manager's questions from the lab's data (Arabic and English)

🏭Production and quality5

  1. 9Production board with stages: design → milling → sintering → finishing → quality check
  2. 10A failed quality check logs a remake and sends the case back to production
  3. 11Technician performance: stages completed, average time, remake rate
  4. 12Shows the lab's slowest stage (the bottleneck)
  5. 13Upload the design (STL, image or PDF) and the doctor approves it in a 3D viewer

💰Invoicing and accounts10

  1. 14Itemized tax invoices with sequential numbering, in Arabic and English
  2. 15Tax for 55 countries: rate, tax name, tax-number label and currency per country
  3. 16Saudi ZATCA QR code (Phase 1) on invoices
  4. 17Credit notes with a reason and the original invoice number; invoices can't be edited after issue
  5. 18Full payment ledger: amount, method, date and who recorded it
  6. 19Service price list, with special prices per doctor
  7. 20Automatic invoice on delivery, or one consolidated invoice per doctor
  8. 21Doctor accounts: balances and debt aging (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days)
  9. 22Printable statement of account per doctor with a running balance
  10. 23Case cost and profitability by service and by doctor

📦Inventory and staff6

  1. 24Inventory per branch with a minimum level and a low-stock alert
  2. 25Materials are deducted from stock automatically on delivery
  3. 26A file for every employee: ID or residency permit, contract, emergency contact
  4. 27Alert 30 days before an ID or residency permit expires
  5. 28Leave balances and leave requests approved by the manager
  6. 29Financial, operational, inventory and staff reports, with PDF printing

📥Import and export3

  1. 30Import inventory and the price list from an Excel or CSV file
  2. 31Ready Excel template, row preview before saving, errors shown by row number
  3. 32Export balances, the price list and technician performance to Excel

⚙️Automations (on/off in Settings)4

  1. 33Weekly report for the lab owner every Sunday
  2. 34Weekly reminders to doctors with overdue balances, plus a summary for the accountant
  3. 35Alert when a case is stuck in a stage longer than usual
  4. 36Monthly statement for every doctor in the portal

👨‍⚕️Doctor portal5

  1. 37Doctors send a new case and track it without creating an account
  2. 38A direct link for every lab; the portal remembers the doctor's lab
  3. 39A doctor account with all their cases, balance and statement
  4. 40Notifications to the doctor on status changes and messages
  5. 41Spam and bot protection on requests

😁Smile designer for doctors6

  1. 42Finds the mouth in the patient's photo automatically
  2. 43Realistic new upper and lower teeth replace the old ones
  3. 44Edit any tooth by touch: move, resize, rotate; measurements update live
  4. 45VITA shade per tooth, undo/redo and zoom to the mouth
  5. 46Separate "after" image, before/after comparison and image download
  6. 47Facial features outside the mouth never change

  5. 52Paid ads and a paid verification badge for freelancers
  6. 53Report, block and a moderation panel; data export and account deletion
