A quick guide for anyone who will work on or take over the platform: what exists and what it does

98features

🦷Lab management (ERP)11

  1. 1Case intake and tracking: received → in progress → ready → delivered, with a full log of who changed what and when
  2. 2Live dashboard with the lab's key numbers
  3. 3A due date for every case and a daily alert for overdue cases
  4. 4Assign a case to a technician; technicians see only their own cases
  5. 5A chat on every case between the lab and the doctor
  6. 6Multi-branch: the admin sees all branches, branch staff see only theirs
  7. 7Role-based permissions: admin, branch manager, employee, HR, accountant, technician
  8. 8AI assistant that answers the manager's questions from the lab's data (Arabic and English)
  9. 9Ctrl+K quick search across pages, cases, invoices and doctors
  10. 10Delivery calendar: the week's cases by due date, overdue in red
  11. 11Dark mode, an onboarding guide for new labs and a dashboard with weekly KPIs

🏭Production and quality6

  1. 12Production board with stages: design → milling → sintering → finishing → quality check
  2. 13A failed quality check logs a remake and sends the case back to production
  3. 14Technician performance: stages completed, average time, remake rate
  4. 15Shows the lab's slowest stage (the bottleneck)
  5. 16QR label on every case: scan it with a phone to open the case and move it to the next stage in one tap
  6. 17Upload the design (STL, image or PDF) and the doctor approves it in a 3D viewer

💰Invoicing and accounts13

  1. 18Itemized tax invoices with sequential numbering, in Arabic and English
  2. 19Tax for 55 countries: rate, tax name, tax-number label and currency per country
  3. 20Saudi ZATCA QR code (Phase 1) on invoices
  4. 21Credit notes with a reason and the original invoice number; invoices can't be edited after issue
  5. 22Full payment ledger: amount, method, date and who recorded it
  6. 23Service price list, with special prices per doctor
  7. 24Automatic invoice on delivery, or one consolidated invoice per doctor
  8. 25Doctor accounts: balances and debt aging (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days)
  9. 26Printable statement of account per doctor with a running balance
  10. 27Case cost and profitability by service and by doctor
  11. 28Doctor loyalty points: points on every payment, Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers, redeemable as an invoice discount
  12. 29Monthly report per doctor: PDF with the lab logo and a WhatsApp summary
  13. 30Email an invoice to the doctor in one click

  1. 31Income statement net of VAT, compared with the previous period

  3. 45Daily attendance sheet for HR, a one-click mark-absent, and a monthly summary per employee
  4. 46“My HR”: employees request leave and see their balance and payslips
  5. 47Annual leave in working days, with weekends and public holidays, and a balance that grows every month
  6. 48Monthly payroll: basic, allowances, piece work from production steps, bonus and overtime

  10. 52Printable payslips and payroll sheet
